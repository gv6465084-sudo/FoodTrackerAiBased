Meal Planning Tips

  • Prep ingredients in advance on weekends
  • Cook in batches and store portions
  • Keep healthy snacks readily available
  • Balance your plate: 1/2 vegetables, 1/4 protein, 1/4 whole grains
  • Stay hydrated - drink water before meals
  • Adjust portions based on your calorie goals
